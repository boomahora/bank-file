# EXTRACT — Revolving Facility Agreement (FICTIONAL, for the worked example)

Northbank N.V. and Meridian Components B.V., dated 12 June 2024.

Clauses reproduced as they would appear. Everything here is invented.

**2.1 The Facility.** The Lender makes available to the Borrower a revolving credit facility
in an aggregate amount of EUR 40,000,000 (the "Facility"), terminating on 12 June 2027 (the
"Termination Date").

**4.3 Cancellation.** The Borrower may cancel the whole or any part of the Facility by giving
the Lender not less than 90 days' prior written notice before the Termination Date. For the
avoidance of doubt, where no such notice is given, the Facility shall be extended for a
further period of 12 months on the terms then applying.

**8.2 Margin.** The Margin shall be 1.85 per cent per annum over EURIBOR.

**8.4 Commitment fee.** The Borrower shall pay a commitment fee computed at the rate of 0.65
per cent per annum on the undrawn, uncancelled amount of the Facility.

**21.1 Financial covenant.** The Borrower shall ensure that Net Leverage does not exceed 3.0:1
at each Quarter Date.
